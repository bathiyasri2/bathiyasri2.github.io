About

What we cover, who we are for, and who hosts.

Signal & Static explores long-form conversations with makers, musicians, and people who think in frequencies. Each episode is edited for clarity, not clickbait.

The show is built for curious listeners who want substance over hot takes—commuters, night owls, and lifelong learners.

Mara Ellison hosts every episode, asking the questions that do not fit in a tweet thread.
